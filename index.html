10. Natal (Birth) Planetary Positions

Method: Positions computed in-browser from orbital elements (accuracy about 1-2 arcminutes). Rahu/Ketu = Mean Node. Ayanamsa = Lahiri. Whole-sign houses from Lagna. Gochar results counted from natal Moon sign. Divisional charts (vargas) use Parashara (BPHS) rules from the sidereal longitude of each planet and the Lagna; D60 and other deep vargas are very sensitive to birth time (a few minutes shifts D60 Lagna). Vimshottari (120 yr) and Yogini (36 yr) dashas are computed from the sidereal (Lahiri) Moon: the nakshatra gives the starting lord and the portion already elapsed gives the balance at birth. Antardasha and Pratyantardasha lengths are proportional (major period x sub-period years / cycle total) and start with the major/sub lord itself. Dates are shown in the birth time zone. General indications only; Vedha, Ashtakavarga and Dasha can modify results.
